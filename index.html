MITRE ATT&CK, ATLAS and mapping data

Pulls the official ATT&CK STIX 2.1 bundles from mitre-attack/attack-stix-data and the ATLAS release files from mitre-atlas/atlas-data into a local database in this browser. The Threat Profile tab reads adversaries (ATT&CK groups, software and campaigns; ATLAS case studies, entities and software), techniques, mitigations and detection strategies from here. It also pulls the Center for Threat-Informed Defense's mappings to ATT&CK techniques of NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5 controls, from center-for-threat-informed-defense/mappings-explorer, which the Threat Profile tab shows beside its results.
